What is Databricks Auto Loader, and how does it work? 

A job that usually runs in 10 minutes is suddenly taking much longer. How would you investigate and troubleshoot the issue? 

If a Databricks job is running very slowly, what steps would you take to improve its performance? 

What is the difference between SCD Type 1 and SCD Type 2? When would you use each? 

What is data skewness in Spark? How can you identify and resolve skewed data? 

What is Liquid Clustering in Databricks, and what are its benefits? 

Why is Liquid Clustering generally not recommended for small tables? 

How is data physically stored when using Liquid Clustering compared to partitioning?

How do you gather and understand business requirements before starting development?
